# Production-Support Runbook

## Who handles support
One developer is on call per week (rotation). Others are not interrupted unless the on-call developer escalates.

## Urgent (interrupt planned work)
- Patients can't book or cancel
- Reminders sent wrongly (e.g. after cancellation)
- Any patient-data or privacy issue
→ Switch to a short sync call, fix first, then blameless RCA.

## Not urgent (add to Backlog)
- Display/text issues, single-clinic configuration questions

## Steps
1. Create an issue with label `support`
2. On-call developer triages within 2 hours
3. Post status in the daily update
